Delete Your Account

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

This page explains how to delete your I'm the Commish account and the data associated with it. You can delete your account directly in the app, or request deletion by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App (Fastest)

  1. Open I'm the Commish and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm that you want to delete your account.

Deletion is immediate. Once you confirm, your account is removed and you are signed out of the app.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have the app installed or are unable to sign in, you can request deletion by email. Send a message to:

Email: [Click to reveal]

Please include the following so we can locate your account:

  • Subject line: Delete my account
  • The username shown on the Profile tab of the app

Deletion requests sent by email are processed within 30 days.

What Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your account and profile, including your username, your email address (if you provided one), and your device identifier
  • Your picks
  • Your contest memberships and any chat messages you posted
  • Your notification preferences and push notification tokens

What May Be Retained

  • Anonymized aggregate contest results and standings for contests you participated in. Your username is removed from these records.
  • Server logs and backups, which may contain your data for up to 30 days after deletion. After that period they are purged.

Questions

For more details on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about account deletion, reach out using the email address above or visit our Support page.
